The CSV file should have the required columns: publish_date, platform, content
CSV Format Requirements

Your CSV file must include these columns:

  • publish_date: Date to publish (YYYY-MM-DD)
  • platform: Platform to publish to (twitter, linkedin, substack)
  • content: The content to publish

Optional columns:

  • title: Title (required for Substack)
  • subtitle: Subtitle (for Substack)
  • url: URL to share (for LinkedIn)
  • publish_time: Time to publish (HH:MM, default: 09:00)
Sample CSV Format
publish_date,platform,content,title,subtitle,url
2023-12-01,twitter,"Just launched our new product! #excited",,
2023-12-01,linkedin,"Excited to announce our new product launch!","New Product Launch",,"https://example.com"
2023-12-01,substack,"<h1>New Product Launch</h1><p>We're excited to announce...</p>","New Product Launch","Exciting news for our customers"
